Privacy Policy

Sprinkler Blowout Helper App
Last Updated: August 7, 2026

Overview

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Sprinkler Blowout Helper is designed with an Offline-First philosophy. All your customer profiles, service records, and job photos are stored locally on your device.

Information Collection & Use

We do not collect, transmit, upload, or sell any of your personal data to remote servers. All operations are 100% local.

To provide specific offline features, the app requests the following device permissions:

  • Location Services (GPS): Used solely to help you pick the exact customer house or main valve location on the map, and to insert GPS coordinates in watermarks on liability photos. This info never leaves your phone.
  • Camera & Photo Library: Used to capture and attach photos of existing property damage or valve conditions. Photos are saved directly into the app's local sandbox container.

Data Storage and Protection

All data (customer profiles, zone settings, pricing formulas, and blowout history) is saved in standard JSON files within the application's secure sandbox. Since we do not use cloud database services, you are in full control of your data.

If you delete the app, all stored data and associated photos will be permanently deleted from your device.

Data Backup and Migration

The app provides a manual data backup utility in the Settings tab. You can export a text-based JSON backup of your records to the system clipboard and paste it elsewhere for safekeeping. No background automated cloud syncing is performed.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding our privacy practices, please contact us via our support channels.
