Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Why can't I see my location on the map?
Please ensure that Location Services are enabled on your iPhone (Settings > Privacy > Location Services) and that Sprinkler Blowout Helper has "While Using the App" authorization.
2. Where are my field photos saved?
Photos taken within the app are saved securely inside the app's local sandbox data container. They will not clutter your personal photo library unless you choose to share them manually.
3. How do I backup my customer records?
Go to the Settings tab, scroll down to the "Data Backup & Recovery" section, and click "Export JSON Backup (Copy)". This copies the text-based backup to your clipboard, allowing you to save it in your Notes or send it via email.
4. How can I customize the pricing template?
Navigate to the Settings tab. Under "Pricing Templates", you can adjust the Base Zone Limit, Base Price, Rate Per Extra Zone, and Booster Pump Surcharge to suit your business model.
