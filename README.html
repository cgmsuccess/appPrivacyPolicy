PillMate Privacy Policy

Your Privacy & Health Data Remain 100% On Your Device

Effective Date: July 31, 2026

1. 100% Offline & Zero Data Collection

PillMate is designed as a standalone, offline daily habit and medication reminder utility. We do NOT operate external servers and do NOT collect, transmit, track, or share any personal health data, medication schedules, or dosage history. All data created within PillMate remains 100% strictly on your local iOS device (`FileManager.default`).

2. Device Permissions Usage

PillMate requests minimal permissions to provide its care tools:

  • Camera Permission: Used exclusively on-device for the Vision OCR Large Text Reader and flashlight illumination. Camera frames are processed in real-time in temporary memory and are never recorded, saved, or uploaded.
  • Notification Permission: Used solely to schedule local alarm chimes on your iPhone. Notification triggers are scheduled locally via `UNUserNotificationCenter`.

3. Third-Party Services & Tracking

PillMate contains zero third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising frameworks. We do not sell or monetize your personal information.

4. Children's Privacy

PillMate complies with COPPA and global privacy regulations. Since we collect no personal data whatsoever, PillMate is completely safe for users of all ages.

5. Medical Disclaimer

PillMate is a personal habit reminder tool and does not constitute a medical device. It provides no medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Users must follow their healthcare provider's professional instructions.
